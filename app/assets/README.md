Refined editorial travel brand, very subtle tactile paper softness but essentially crisp flat shapes. No outer rounded square or border, no phone mockup, no shadows outside the emblem, no watermark. Central artwork must fit well inside the central 65 percent safe area. Opaque full canvas.
