> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app icon, square 1024x1024 full-bleed opaque PNG. Primary request: create an elegant Korean photography location recommendation app icon for PhotoSpot. Sophisticated warm ivory background #F6F3EE, deep muted slate blue emblem #536882 and a subtle terracotta accent. Refined editorial travel brand, very subtle tactile paper softness but essentially crisp flat shapes. No outer rounded square or border, no phone mockup, no shadows outside the emblem, no watermark. Central artwork must fit well inside the central 65 percent safe area. Opaque full canvas.
